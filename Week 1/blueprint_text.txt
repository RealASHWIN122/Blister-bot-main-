Intelligent  Edge-AI  Medicine  Dispenser  
Hardware  Platform:  Arduino  UNO  Q  (Qualcomm  Dragonwing  QRB2210  +  STM32U585  MCU)

1.  Project  Overview  &  Architectural  Philosophy  
The  Intelligent  Edge-AI  Medicine  Dispenser  is  an  autonomous,  fully  offline  medication  
management

It

original
 
uncut
 
blister

The  system  is  built  upon  the  dual-processor  architecture  of  the  Arduino  UNO  Q .  This

(AI)

(CV)
 
workloads

hard

mechanical
 
control:
 ●  The  Linux  MPU  (Qualcomm  Dragonwing):  Handles  natural  language  processing,  
localized
 
Large
 
Language
 
Models
 
(LLMs),
 
database
 
management,
 
and
 
intensive

 ●  The  STM32  Microcontroller  (MCU):  Dedicated  exclusively  to  reliable,  precise  motor

D.  Vision-Guided  Deblistering  Mechanics  (Core  Innovation)

Front-to-Back  Coordinate  Mapping:  The

the
 
--- PAGE ---
exact  geometric  position  of  each  tablet,
