# Week 4: Edge AI Prototyping

During Week 4 (Aug 18 - Aug 24), we explored numerous AI models in isolation to determine what would be viable on our edge computing device (Uno Q).

## Accomplishments
- **OCR Modules**: Evaluated text extraction mechanisms for reading blister pack labels.
- **Speech Models**: Built `texttospeech` and `speechtotext` standalone scripts to test offline inference speeds.
- **Action Recognition**: Tested the Edge Impulse action recognition platform (`action-recog-applab`).
- **Hardware Inspiration**: Sourced and reviewed the `Arduino mini CNC plotter` project as a mechanical base for our drilling mechanism.
