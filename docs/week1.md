# Week 1: Initial Mechanical Design & Ideation

During Week 1 (July 28 - Aug 3), we laid the groundwork for the mechanical operation of the Blister Bot.

## Accomplishments
- Explored 3D models and animations to visualize how a machine could automatically punch a pill out of a blister pack.
- Documented initial CAD and scoring ideas.
- Designed early iterations of the scoring mechanisms (cutting the foil before plunging).

All historical files from Week 1 (Blender models, animations, old reports) are archived in [reference/week1/](../reference/week1/).
