# Packet paddle wheel

- Single integrated rotor; Z is the hub axis. Four blades repeat at 90°.
- Blade length 95 mm parallel to Z; base thickness exactly 3 mm. Outer blade width 68 mm includes two 2 mm rail walls, with exactly 64 mm between the internal vertical walls.
- Each centered rectangular window is 50 × 80 mm, through the base. Centering leaves 7.5 mm flush end borders. The loading-end border is not raised and does not block axial sliding; an entirely absent entry border would require an additional notch or a non-centered/open-ended window.
- Loading is from Z=0 toward the raised stop at Z=93–95 mm. Each pair of inward-facing channels has 2 mm retaining lips and 3.5 mm clearance above the base; top opening between lips is 60 mm. No raised transverse loading-end obstruction.
- Assumed hub Ø20 × 97 mm (Z=-1 to 96 mm), rail height 5 mm above base, wall thickness 2 mm, lip thickness 1.5 mm.
- Socket: nominal Ø5 mm circle truncated at X=1.5 mm (4 mm from flat to opposite circular extreme); blind depth 12 mm from Z=-1. No fit allowance added. Verify flat and clearance against the actual motor shaft before fabrication.
- Rectangle profiles share a dimension-driven helper function because the same construction repeats at differing sizes. Its sketches are not point-and-click editable; dimensions remain editable in source.
- Validation: all seven evaluated solver sketches fully constrained; lint passed; rendered overview and close-ups verified windows, guide entrances, stops, and D-shaped cavity.
